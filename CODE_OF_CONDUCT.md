# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive
feedback, and remember that people of every experience level (developers,
accountants and first-time contributors) are here to learn and help.

Harassment, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not
tolerated.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the
contact details on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.
